The standard

What has to be true before a home is published

This is a rule we keep, not a ratio we measured. It applies to the first home exactly as it will to the hundredth.

  1. It is a home we manage

    Nomad is not a listing site. A home appears here only when we hold the management agreement for it, so the person answering your message is the person responsible for the house.

  2. The page is checked before it goes up

    Bedroom count, bed configuration, bathrooms, parking, the road in, the things that are awkward about the place. A home stays unpublished until every one of those is confirmed rather than assumed.

  3. The price on the page is the price

    One nightly rate, the cleaning fee, tax, and one 4% booking fee instead of the 14.1–16.5% a marketplace adds. No resort fee, and nothing that appears for the first time on the last screen.

  4. The policy is published with it

    Cancellation terms, payment schedule and house rules are on the page before you book, in full, not summarized into a tier name.

  5. It comes down if it stops being true

    If something changes at a house and we cannot describe it accurately, the home is unpublished until we can. An unpublished home is not counted anywhere on this site.

A home that no longer meets the standard is unpublished until it does. Owners can read the same standard from the other side.
