Why here

What San Diego is actually good at

The most stable weather in America

Highs between 65 and 78°F almost every month. May and June bring marine layer mornings that burn off by noon.

Surf for every level

Tourmaline for longboards, PB Point on a west swell, Blacks for experts, and gentle bay water in Mission Bay for kids.

Balboa Park and the zoo

1,200 acres of gardens, seventeen museums, and the world's best-known zoo, ten minutes from downtown.

Baja on the doorstep

Valle de Guadalupe wine country and Puerto Nuevo lobster are a 90-minute drive south with a passport.

Neighborhoods

Where to stay in San Diego

Pacific Beach

Three miles of boardwalk, surf shops, and the best casual food-to-sand ratio in the county.

La Jolla

Sea caves, sea lions, cliff walks, and the county's most refined village dining.

Mission Beach

Ocean on one side, calm bay on the other. Best for families who want both.

Little Italy

Downtown's best restaurant district and Saturday market, walkable to the waterfront.

Gaslamp Quarter

Victorian downtown blocks, Petco Park at the edge, nightlife until 2am.

Ask us something else
Which San Diego neighborhood is best for families?

Mission Beach for bay-side calm water and the boardwalk, Coronado for wide flat sand, or Pacific Beach's quieter north end. Downtown and the Gaslamp are better for adults.

What is May Gray and June Gloom?

A marine layer that keeps coastal mornings overcast in late spring, usually clearing by early afternoon. Inland neighborhoods are sunny the whole time.

Is parking difficult at the beach?

In summer, yes. Any listing here should state exactly how many off-street spaces you get, and whether they are garage, driveway or permit street spots — ours do.
