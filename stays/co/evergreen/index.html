Vacation rentals in Evergreen, Colorado

Evergreen sits at 7,220 feet in the ponderosa belt above Denver, where the plains give up and the Front Range begins. It is a working mountain town, not a resort: an elk herd on the golf course, a lake loop that freezes hard enough to skate in January, and a main street of outfitters and coffee roasters that has resisted being cute. Our Evergreen homes are chosen for the thing people actually come for — a deck facing west, a stove that throws real heat, and enough quiet that you can hear snow come off the roof.

Why here

What Evergreen is actually good at

Red Rocks in 25 minutes

The amphitheatre is a straight shot down Bear Creek and Highway 74 — close enough to make a weeknight show, far enough that you sleep in silence afterward.

Four honest seasons

Aspen gold in late September, deep dry powder January through March, wildflower meadows in July. Shoulder seasons here are a feature, not a discount.

Trailheads, not lift lines

Elk Meadow, Three Sisters, Alderfer, and Mount Falcon all start within fifteen minutes of our homes. No pass, no parking reservation, no crowds by 9am.

Denver access without Denver noise

Downtown is 35 minutes on I-70 in normal traffic. DEN is under an hour. It is the rare mountain town you can actually commute from.

Neighborhoods

Where to stay in Evergreen

Bergen Park

The high shelf above town — bigger lots, longer views, the last grocery store before the pines close in.

Downtown Evergreen

Walkable to the lake, the creek, and eight restaurants. Best for guests who would rather not drive after dinner.

Conifer

Fifteen minutes south and 400 feet higher. Quieter, woodsier, closer to Staunton State Park.

Idaho Springs

Historic mining canyon on I-70 with hot springs and the fastest access to the high peaks.

Upper Bear Creek

The valley west of Evergreen Lake, where Bear Creek runs upstream into Clear Creek County and the lots turn to wooded acreage.

Ask us something else
Do I need four-wheel drive in Evergreen?

From December through March, yes — or at minimum snow tires. County plows clear the main roads quickly, but our homes above Bergen Park sit on steep private drives that can hold ice for days. Every listing page states the driveway grade and whether AWD is required.

How long is the drive from Denver airport to Evergreen?

Typically 55–70 minutes via I-70 West to Exit 252. Add 20 minutes during a Friday afternoon snowstorm. We text arrival directions with live road conditions the morning of check-in.

Can I get to a Red Rocks show and back the same night?

Easily. It is 25–30 minutes each way via Highway 74 and Morrison. We include a Red Rocks night-of guide with parking lot recommendations in the house manual.

Is altitude a problem at 7,200 feet?

Mild for most guests — expect a headache and poor sleep the first night if you have flown in from sea level. Drink far more water than feels necessary, skip the first-night bottle of wine, and go easy on day one hikes.
