The direct way to stay

Same home, same dates, one fewer fee

A marketplace adds a guest service fee at checkout — Airbnb publishes it as 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal. Ours is 4%, which is 10.1–12.5 percentage points of the booking you keep. Here is exactly what changes when you book on this site instead.

4% booking fee, not 14.1–16.5%

Marketplaces add a guest service fee at checkout — Airbnb publishes a range of 14.1–16.5% of the booking subtotal. Ours is 4% of the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee, which is 10.1–12.5 percentage points of the booking that stays with you. What you pay is the nightly rate, the cleaning fee, applicable taxes, and any add-on you asked for.

One price, itemized before you pay

Every line is on the page: rate, nights, cleaning, tax, total. Nothing appears at the last step, and nothing is folded into a nightly rate to make a headline number look smaller.

You are talking to the company that manages the home

Not a listing platform passing a message to a host. The same people who write the listing hold the keys, so a question about the house has one answer and one person behind it.

The policies are published in full

Cancellation terms, house rules and what happens if something breaks are written on the page before you book, in plain language, not summarized into a tier name.

How this is calculated

Assumption: the nightly rate and the cleaning fee are identical on both sides. This compares fees, not prices. If a marketplace listing of the same home is priced differently, the comparison does not hold — check both totals yourself before you decide.

  • Our own booking fee — 4% of the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee, charged by our booking platform and shown as “Service fee” on the checkout panel. Taken from a real quote on this site on 2026-08-15, not from a rate card. It is the one fee we add, and it is in the total before you reach the card screen.
  • Airbnb guest service fee — 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal. This is the range we apply, which is why the result below is a range and not a single number. airbnb.com/help/article/1857
  • Vrbo — 5% commission plus 3% payment processing, charged on the host side rather than at your checkout. help.vrbo.com
  • Booking.com — varies by agreement. No universal rate is published, so we do not put a number on it. partner.booking.com

Figures checked 2026-08-07. Taxes are the same either way and are excluded from the comparison.

How pricing works

You are quoted the nightly rate, the cleaning fee, applicable lodging tax and any add-on you selected. 4% booking fee, not 14.1–16.5% is added at any step, and the nightly rate is not raised to absorb one.

Payment and security

Payment is taken over TLS on the PCI-certified checkout our booking platform provides. Nomad never sees, handles or stores your card number. Which company appears as the merchant on your statement depends on the platform plan the home is on, and it is named on your confirmation.

Source: help.hospitable.com/en/articles/14631693-fraud-protection-and-chargebacks-for-direct-bookings

Cancellation

Cancellation terms are set per home and printed in full on that home's page and in your confirmation email. There is no tier name to decode — you will see the dates and the amounts.

Until a home is published, its terms are not final and are not shown.

House rules

Every home publishes its own rules — occupancy, quiet hours, pets, events, parking — on its page, before booking rather than after arrival.
