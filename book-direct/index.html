The direct way to stay

Why booking direct is cheaper — with the sources attached

Airbnb publishes a guest service fee of 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal, added to your total at checkout. Ours is 4% of the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee — 10.1–12.5 percentage points of the booking that stays with you instead. That is the whole argument, and every number used to make it on this page links to the company that publishes it.

How this is calculated

Assumption: the nightly rate and the cleaning fee are identical on both sides. This compares fees, not prices. If a marketplace listing of the same home is priced differently, the comparison does not hold — check both totals yourself before you decide.

  • Our own booking fee — 4% of the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee, charged by our booking platform and shown as “Service fee” on the checkout panel. Taken from a real quote on this site on 2026-08-15, not from a rate card. It is the one fee we add, and it is in the total before you reach the card screen.
  • Airbnb guest service fee — 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal. This is the range we apply, which is why the result below is a range and not a single number. airbnb.com/help/article/1857
  • Vrbo — 5% commission plus 3% payment processing, charged on the host side rather than at your checkout. help.vrbo.com
  • Booking.com — varies by agreement. No universal rate is published, so we do not put a number on it. partner.booking.com

Figures checked 2026-08-07. Taxes are the same either way and are excluded from the comparison.

One price, itemized before you pay

You are talking to the company that manages the home

The policies are published in full

Cancellation terms, house rules and what happens if something breaks are written on the page before you book, in plain language, not summarized into a tier name.

The trade-offs

Ask us something else
Is booking direct with Nomad cheaper?

It replaces the guest service fee that marketplaces add at checkout with a smaller one. Airbnb publishes that fee as 14.1–16.5% of the booking subtotal; Vrbo publishes a 5% commission plus 3% payment processing on the host side. Ours is 4% of the nightly rate plus the cleaning fee — 10.1–12.5 percentage points of the booking that stays with you — and we do not raise the nightly rate to absorb anything. Whether your total is lower also depends on the nightly rate itself, which is why our calculator states its assumption and shows a range rather than one number.

Do you have guest reviews?

No. Nomad Vacation Rentals LLC was founded in 2026 and has not hosted a stay yet. We would rather tell you that than show you reviews we wrote. When reviews exist, each one will be attached to a booking we can verify.

Is my payment secure?

Payment is taken over TLS on the PCI-certified checkout our booking platform provides, so Nomad never sees, handles or stores your card number. You receive a payment receipt and a Nomad confirmation with the full line-item breakdown.

What is your cancellation policy?

It is set per home and published in full on that home's page and in your confirmation, with the dates and the amounts written out. We do not use tier names.

Who am I actually dealing with?

Nomad Vacation Rentals LLC, founded in 2026 by Sean Normoyle. He is the named accountable person for every decision on this site.

How many homes do you have?

The count on the portfolio page is generated from published homes and is always current. Homes being prepared do not appear anywhere as inventory, and we will not describe a home as available before it is.
