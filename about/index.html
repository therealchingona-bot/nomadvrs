About Nomad

A new company, saying so plainly.

Nomad Vacation Rentals LLC was formed in 2026 to rent vacation homes directly, from the company that manages them, with one 4% booking fee instead of the 14.1–16.5% a marketplace adds. That is the whole idea. Everything below is either a fact about the company or a description of how we intend to work — and we have tried hard to keep those two things clearly apart.

How we operate

Three rules we do not bend

Say the hard thing on the listing page

Steep driveway, no air conditioning, a loud street on Saturday, a 1921 staircase. A real limitation described in advance is forgiven; the same one discovered at 11pm is not.

Publish nothing we cannot show you

No review we did not receive, no statistic we did not measure, no guarantee we have not written down. This site is built so that an unverified claim fails the build rather than reaching the page.

One price, itemized

The total is the total: rate, cleaning, tax and one 4% booking fee instead of the 14.1–16.5% a marketplace adds. No resort fee, nothing that appears on the last screen.

Why we exist

Two things happen when a vacation rental only exists on a marketplace. The guest pays a service fee on top of the rate the owner set — Airbnb publishes it as 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal — and nobody in the transaction has a relationship worth protecting. The platform mediates every conversation, so a broken water heater becomes a support ticket rather than a call to someone who can send a plumber.

Nomad exists to remove the middle: we manage the homes, we rent them to you directly, and the total you are quoted is the total you pay, with one 4% booking fee instead of the 14.1–16.5% a marketplace adds. That is not a claim about how well we do it. It is a description of the arrangement, and you can check every part of it on this site before you spend anything.

Nomad is small. Sean hosts some of these homes himself, and for the others he works with a local team he chose and works alongside — they handle cleaning and turnovers, maintenance and repairs, and guest messages. So the person who answers you may be Sean or it may be one of them, and either way it is someone who can get to the house, not a call center in another state. What does not change is that you are dealing with the people responsible for the home you booked rather than a platform sitting between you.

What we are not

Not a platform, not a franchise, and not a technology company that happens to hold keys. Not affiliated with Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com. And not an established operator — if a long track record is what you need before you book with a company, that is an entirely reasonable position, and we would rather you took it than felt misled into a first stay.

What we will not put on this website

Reviews we did not receive. A star rating we did not earn. A response-time figure we have not measured — Sean will tell you himself that he replies quickly, and that is his word rather than a statistic. A guarantee nobody has drafted or underwritten. A revenue figure for an owner whose home we have not seen. A phone number or an address that does not connect to anyone. Every one of those is easy to write and impossible to take back, and each of them was on this site before this page was rewritten.

What we publish instead is the fee arithmetic with its sources attached, the standard a home has to meet before it appears here, and the policies in full before you book.

  • The all-in total, itemized, before the payment step — not a nightly rate that grows at checkout.

How pricing works

You are quoted the nightly rate, the cleaning fee, applicable lodging tax and any add-on you selected. 4% booking fee, not 14.1–16.5% is added at any step, and the nightly rate is not raised to absorb one.

Payment and security

Payment is taken over TLS on the PCI-certified checkout our booking platform provides. Nomad never sees, handles or stores your card number. Which company appears as the merchant on your statement depends on the platform plan the home is on, and it is named on your confirmation.

Source: help.hospitable.com/en/articles/14631693-fraud-protection-and-chargebacks-for-direct-bookings

Cancellation

Cancellation terms are set per home and printed in full on that home's page and in your confirmation email. There is no tier name to decode — you will see the dates and the amounts.

Until a home is published, its terms are not final and are not shown.

House rules

Every home publishes its own rules — occupancy, quiet hours, pets, events, parking — on its page, before booking rather than after arrival.
